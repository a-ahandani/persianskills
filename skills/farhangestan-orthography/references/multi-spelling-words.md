# Words with more than one spelling (book pp. 72–85)

Source: «دستور خطّ فارسی (ویراست جدید)»، فرهنگستان زبان و ادب فارسی. The
book gives the Academy's preferred spelling («املای مرجّح») for current words
that have two or more written forms: common words on pp. 73–81 (about 134
entries) and proper names on pp. 82–85 (about 55). This file explains how to
use the lists and gives a sample. It does not reproduce them; look a word up
on those pages of the official PDF.

## The policy (p. 72)

- The lists cover words used in today's Persian that have more than one
  spelling, and widely used names of plants, animals, foods and medicines.
- Obsolete and rare words are left out and keep the spelling of the text they
  appear in.
- Old texts keep their recorded form of a word or name even when the
  Academy's preferred form differs.
- The Academy chose by, in order: the more common spelling; history and not
  creating more homographs; never proposing an unusual spelling.

## Reading an entry

- Right column: the existing spellings. Left column: the preferred one.
- One form on the left: that form is preferred; correct the others to it.
- Two forms on the left: both are accepted; don't change one to the other.
  At most, suggest one form consistently within a document.
- A gloss in parentheses can split one pair by meaning. Check it first:
  «طاق» (vault) but «تاق» (odd, as opposed to even); «تراز» (level) but
  «طراز» (embroidery, and «طراز اوّل»).
- Rows sort by the first existing form, which is often the non-preferred one,
  so search under every candidate letter (ت and ط, ق and غ, ه and ح, س and ص,
  ز and ذ).

## What the lists are mostly about

Letters that sound the same, not hamza or alef maqsura:

| Pattern | Sample |
|---|---|
| ت / ط | «اتاق»، «اتو»، «باتری»، «بلیت»، «تالار»، «امپراتور»، «تپیدن» |
| غ / ق | «قورباغه»، «چغندر»، «باجناغ»، «قدّاره»، «یورغه» |
| ز / ذ | «آذوقه»، «زغال» |
| س / ص | «اسطبل» |
| ه / ح | «حلیم»، «حوله» |
| extra «و» | «خشنود»، «سرمه» |

**The choices are not mechanical.** ت usually wins over ط, but «طاس»، «طومار»،
«قوطی»، «لوطی»، «ملاط»، «فطیر» keep ط. ق usually wins in Turkic loans, but
«چغندر»، «زغال»، «سوغات»، «بلغور»، «آروغ» keep غ, and «غدغن» beats «قدغن». So
look entries up; never apply a letter-swap rule to a word that isn't listed.

## Notes the book attaches

- «توفان/ طوفان» are both accepted, but the fixed phrase is «طوفانِ نوح».
- «هم‌تراز» and «هم‌طراز» are both correct.
- Derivatives follow their head where the book says so: «تپیدن» and its
  derivatives with ت; «غلتیدن»، «غلتان»، «غلتک».
- ک/گ pairs such as «اشک/ اشگ»، «لشکر/ لشگر»، «زرشک/ زرشگ» are free variants;
  the book gives no preference.
- Historical titles keep «آغا» («آغامحمّدخان»، «گلین‌آغا») though «آقا» is preferred.
- «پیشخوان/ پیش‌خوان» are both accepted.
- «آمریکا/ امریکا» and «آفریقا/ افریقا» are both accepted.
